What affects the price

The published price is a starting price. I send the exact amount after a call or an audit.

What affects the SEO price

  • Website size — the number of pages and products.
  • Competition in your niche — how strong the competitors on Google's first page are.
  • Scope of work — audit, implementation, content, links.

What affects the website price

  • Package type — one-page site, business website or online store.
  • Add-ons — languages, blog, filters, customer accounts.
  • Content readiness — texts and images also affect the timeline.
