Link building and backlinks

Links from other websites are one of the signals Google uses to judge whether a page can be trusted. What matters is not the number of links, but where they come from and whether they are relevant to your industry.

How I build links

I follow Google's spam policies. Every link is earned through real partnerships and content.

Topical relevance

A link from a car blog will not help a medical clinic. I select sources based on your niche.

Source quality

I assess a site's authority (Domain Rating) and organic traffic, not just whether a link can be obtained.

Natural anchor text

Too many keywords in links can do harm. I keep a natural balance between the brand name and search phrases.

Content worth referencing

Articles and materials that other websites want to publish or cite — so the link appears in a natural context.

Local directories

Consistent business details in Latvian directories — this also helps local SEO.

Try a sample: keep or disavow?

Illustration

Six made-up examples. Choose a decision for each link — then you will see how it would be judged in an audit.

  • directory.example.lv

    Industry directory: business name, address and phone match your details, link from the business profile page.

    Keep. The source fits the industry, the details match, and the link sits in a natural place.

  • local-portal.example.lv

    An article on a local news portal that mentions the business with a link to the website.

    Keep. A real mention in local media is exactly what Google looks for.

  • forum-8812.example.lv

    Unrelated forum: hundreds of identical comments using the same keyword as the link text.

    Disavow. No connection to the industry and repeated anchor text — a typical sign of link spam.

  • partner.example.lv

    A supplier's website: your business is listed in the partners section.

    Keep. A genuine business relationship that also makes sense to a reader.

  • links-for-money.example.lv

    A site that openly sells links, referenced by hundreds of unrelated businesses.

    Disavow. Links bought to influence rankings go against Google's spam policy.

  • auto-texts.example.lv

    An auto-generated site with randomly assembled text and links to hundreds of unrelated pages.

    Disavow. The content is not made for people, and the link is not a real recommendation.

This is how I review a link profile in an audit: each source is judged by industry relevance, context and whether the link looks natural.

Illustration: the domains are made up. The disavow tool is meant for rare cases — Google ignores most obviously worthless links by itself, so it is used only when there are many of them or a manual action has been received.

Questions about link building

What is a backlink?

A backlink is a link from another website to your website. Google treats it as a recommendation: the more relevant and trustworthy the source, the more the link is worth.

Can links be bought?

Links bought to influence rankings go against Google's spam policies and can harm a website. I work only with partnerships and content.

How many links do you need?

There is no universal number. It depends on your competitors' link profiles in your niche — I compare them in the audit.
