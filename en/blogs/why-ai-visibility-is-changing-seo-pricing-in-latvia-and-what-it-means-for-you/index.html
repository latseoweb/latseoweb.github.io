Why AI Visibility Is Changing SEO Pricing in Latvia (and What It Means for You)

Author: Adrians Stankevics•~3 min read

The Most Common Mistake Latvian Clients Make That Unfairly Lowers Prices

In my experience, the biggest mistake SEO clients in Latvia make is not understanding the true necessity of SEO today. Especially after the recent massive digital shifts driven by AI, SEO has become more important than ever. It now directly affects whether you'll be found in AI chats like ChatGPT or Gemini.

But many clients don't grasp this value. They think, "You're just going to insert a few keywords. Why should I pay so much?" The reality is that keywords are only the foundation - the base around which everything else is built. If clients undervalue this, they unfairly push prices down, not realizing the depth of work involved.

How AI Changes the Scope of SEO Work (and Therefore the Price)

The workload has grown significantly. Websites now need to be optimized not only for Google Search but also for AI platforms. Following basic logic, the price must rise to reflect this expanded scope. At LatSEO, we've embraced this shift, and our pricing reflects the added value we deliver.

What Does AI Optimization Actually Involve?

  • Structuring content so AI models can parse and cite it accurately.
  • Implementing technical SEO enhancements that make your site more accessible to AI crawlers.
  • Creating comprehensive content that answers user intent in a way that AI can reference.

How AI Changes Specific SEO Pricing in Latvia

Whether AI optimization is included in the price depends entirely on the client's preferences. If a client doesn't want to be visible in AI results, we can skip that part. However, I personally don't charge extra for AI visibility. Instead, I've seen that my clients' visibility in AI increases by at least 30% as a result of our standard SEO work. This is a significant benefit that comes as part of the package.

What Drives Up the Cost of SEO Projects in Latvia?

Several factors significantly increase the cost of SEO projects here. First, the competitive landscape: we need to conduct deep competitor research to understand how to outrank them. This involves analyzing their strategies, content, and backlinks - a time-consuming process.

Second, the cost of professional SEO tools is substantial. High-quality tools are essential for accurate analysis, keyword research, and performance tracking, and they don't come cheap. These costs are reflected in the project price.

Can You Give a Specific Example with Numbers?

I can't provide a specific price breakdown because I already include AI optimization in my standard pricing. This means you don't have to worry about hidden costs - it's all bundled into a transparent package.

What This Means for You

If you're a business owner in Latvia, understand that SEO is no longer just about Google rankings. It's about being visible everywhere your customers are - including AI platforms. The value is higher than ever, and the pricing reflects that. But at LatSEO, we strive to make it accessible and transparent.
