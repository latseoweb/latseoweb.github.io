What My Video SEO Audits Reveal: The First Mistake I Always Spot

Author: Adrians Stankevics•~3 min read

Why I Record Video Audits for Every Client

I personally conduct video audits for all LatSEO clients and send them the recordings. This approach lets me show exactly what I see when I analyze a website - and it often reveals issues that would otherwise go unnoticed.

The First Mistake I Always Spot: Meta Title and Description

The very first thing I notice in almost every audit is an unoptimized meta title and meta description. These are the absolute basics of SEO, yet so many websites get them wrong. I always point this out to clients and suggest concrete improvements.

For example, I might see a fishing tackle shop using a title like "Best Picks in Latvia" when it should clearly be "Best Fishing Rods in Latvia" or something similar. Tools simply cannot detect this kind of mismatch - it requires human judgment.

Keyword Strength: The Problem That Tools Can't Show

One of the most common issues I uncover is keyword strength levels. Tools cannot directly display this metric because it is partially subjective. I spot these problems based on my own expertise and what I believe is the right approach for each specific page.

This is where a local SEO strategy becomes important - understanding what your actual customers are searching for, not just what a tool suggests.

My Go-To Tool: Ahrefs Toolbar

For video audits, I rely on the Ahrefs Toolbar. It allows me to quickly review all the technical SEO fundamentals without unnecessary hassle. The toolbar gives me instant access to key on-page data while I record my screen and explain findings in real time.

Backlinks: The Concept Clients Always Struggle With

The topic that clients most often fail to understand immediately is backlinks. I always have to explain this concept in greater depth. People hear "backlinks" and think it's just about getting any link, but the quality, relevance, and authority of those links matter far more.

This is why I incorporate backlink analysis into my technical SEO audits - it's not enough to just fix on-page issues if your off-page profile is weak.

What My Video Audits Actually Reveal

In every video audit, I aim to cover three main areas:

  • Meta data optimization - fixing titles and descriptions that don't match search intent
  • Keyword targeting - identifying where the strength level is off and suggesting better alternatives
  • Technical basics - using Ahrefs Toolbar to verify all fundamental elements are in place

The video format is powerful because clients can see exactly what I see. They watch me scroll through their site, point out problems, and explain solutions in plain language. This builds trust and helps them understand why certain changes are necessary.
