SEO mistakes that change your thinking: 5 insights from a LatSEO specialist

Author: Adrians Stankevics•~3 min read

How I Learned That SEO Is Not Just Keywords

Every SEO specialist has made mistakes, but they shape us. In my practice, there was one mistake that completely changed my thinking about technical SEO and attention to detail.

The First Big Mistake: URL Change and 404 Page

One of the first and most painful mistakes was changing URLs without noticing that Google Ads campaigns were targeting the old URL. When I changed the URL, all the ads continued to lead to the old address, which meant potential clients landed on a 404 page.

This mistake taught me that SEO is not just content and keywords - it is also about the entire marketing ecosystem. Today, before making any changes, I always check how it will affect other channels. If you need help with technical SEO, we offer SEO optimization services.

EEAT: The Principle I Ignored for Too Long

My biggest delay was ignoring the EEAT (Expertise, Authority, Trustworthiness) principles. At first, I did not understand its necessity, thinking that well-optimized content would be enough.

When I finally delved into the essence of EEAT, I realized how important it is in Google's ranking system. I immediately got to work and started improving these aspects for all my clients. The results appeared on their own - organic traffic increased, and positions improved.

  • Author biographies and proof of expertise
  • Trustworthy reviews and client ratings
  • Quality external links from authoritative sources

The Role of SEO in Business Decision-Making

SEO itself does not help make business decisions, but it is extremely important for obtaining quality leads. I always emphasize to clients that SEO is a long-term investment that pays off with a steady flow of quality potential clients.

Many entrepreneurs think SEO is just about Google rankings, but in reality, it is about the right people finding your product or service exactly when they are searching for it.

Morning Routine: Google Search Console

Every morning I open Google Search Console (GSC), because I am interested in the overall results of all pages, not just the position of a single keyword. This tool provides a comprehensive view of how the entire website is performing.

I also check individual keywords if it is important to the client and we have set specific goals. This approach allows me to quickly identify problems and opportunities.

How AI Overview Changed My Approach

I change my approach depending on the current situation in the SEO world. The last time this happened was when AI Overview appeared. This Google update forced me to adapt so that my clients would appear in it.

I also started optimizing pages from a GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) perspective, which means content is created so that artificial intelligence tools can understand and use it as well.

The SEO world is constantly changing, and what worked yesterday may not work tomorrow. That is why we at LatSEO always keep up with the latest trends and adapt our strategies. If you want to learn more about our approach, check out our services and prices.

SEO is a continuous learning process, and every mistake is an opportunity to become better. The key is to keep learning and adapting.

Conclusions

  • Always check how changes affect other marketing channels
  • EEAT is a mandatory prerequisite for a successful SEO strategy
  • SEO is a long-term investment for obtaining quality leads
  • GSC provides a comprehensive insight into website health
  • In the AI era, content must be optimized not only for Google but also for artificial intelligence tools
