7 SEO Basics Every Business Owner Must Know Before Building a Website

Author: Adrians Stankevics • ~4 min read

Why SEO Should Matter to You Before You Even Build a Website

Here's the thing: most business owners think about SEO only after their website is live. That's a costly mistake. For example, a bakery in Riga spent €2,500 on a brand-new site, only to discover six months later that none of its pages were indexed by Google. The fix required rebuilding the entire site architecture - a process that cost another €1,500 and three weeks of downtime.

In this listicle, I'll walk you through 7 SEO fundamentals that you need to know before you spend a single euro on development. These are the same principles we apply in our local SEO services, and they can save you thousands in rework.

1. Keyword Research Comes Before Design

You might love how a certain color looks, but if your target customers search for "plumbing services in Liepaja" and your site says "we fix pipes," you'll never show up. Start by listing 10-20 phrases your customers actually type into Google. Use free tools like Google Keyword Planner or AnswerThePublic.

For example, a law firm in Jelgava discovered that "divorce lawyer cost Latvia" had 3,600 monthly searches, while "family attorney" had only 90. By optimizing for the first phrase, they tripled their consultation requests in two months. Skip this step, and you're building a website that no one can find.

2. Site Structure Determines Your Rankings

Think of your website as a filing cabinet. Each page should have a clear purpose, and the main navigation should link to your most important services. A flat structure - where every page is reachable within three clicks - helps Google understand your content.

Take the example of an e-commerce store in Daugavpils that had 500 products buried under five levels of categories. After we simplified the structure to three levels, organic traffic jumped by 67% in just six weeks. This is a core part of our technical SEO service, and it's something you should plan before writing a single line of code.

3. Page Speed Is Non-Negotiable

Google's data shows that 53% of mobile users abandon a page that takes longer than three seconds to load. If your website is slow, you're losing half your potential customers before they even see your content.

A travel agency in Riga lost 40% of its mobile traffic after adding heavy image sliders. After compressing images and enabling caching, page load time dropped from 4.8 seconds to 1.9 seconds. The result? A 28% increase in bookings within a month. Make speed a requirement in your project brief, not an afterthought.

4. Content Is More Than Just Text

Google rewards websites that answer questions thoroughly. That means each page should have at least 300 words of unique, helpful content - not just product descriptions or a paragraph about your company. For example, a dental clinic in Ventspils added 500-word guides on each procedure they offer. Within three months, they ranked for 15 new keywords and saw a 2.5x increase in appointment requests.

Our content strategy service helps you plan and create this kind of content from the ground up. Remember: thin content is the #1 reason small business sites fail to rank.

5. Mobile-First Is the Only Way

Over 60% of all Google searches come from mobile devices. If your site isn't mobile-friendly, you're essentially invisible to a majority of your audience. Google uses the mobile version of your site for indexing and ranking, so a poor mobile experience drags down your desktop rankings too.

A construction company in Ogre lost 70% of its inquiries because its contact form didn't work on phones. Fixing that one issue - plus making buttons larger - led to a 45% increase in quote requests. Build with mobile first, not as an afterthought.

6. Secure and Clean URLs Matter

Simple, readable URLs like yoursite.com/plumbing-services are far better than yoursite.com/index.php?id=342. They tell Google and users what the page is about. Also, never use underscores; use hyphens between words.

For example, changing a URL from /products/item1234 to /products/red-widget helped a hardware store in Valmiera rank for 22 additional keywords in just 10 weeks. Clean URLs are a small change with a big impact, and they're part of our website development best practices.

7. Analytics Must Be Installed from Day One

You can't improve what you can't measure. Install Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console before your site goes live. These free tools show you which pages perform, where your traffic comes from, and what people search for to find you.

A car repair shop in Riga didn't install analytics for six months. When they finally did, they discovered that their blog post about "car AC repair" was getting 500 visits a month - but their homepage was getting 50. Without data, they'd never have known to put that content front and center. Don't make the same mistake.
