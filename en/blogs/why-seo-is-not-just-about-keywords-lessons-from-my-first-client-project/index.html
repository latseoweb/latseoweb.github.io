Why SEO Is Not Just About Keywords: Lessons from My First Client Project

Author: Adrians Stankevics•~2 min read

The Myth of Keyword-Only SEO

When I started working on my first client project for bratus.lv, I made a classic mistake: I focused only on keywords. And guess what? It helped a little, but not enough. It wasn't until I learned about EEAT (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) that I changed my strategy. The results improved dramatically.

This experience taught me a valuable lesson: SEO is a holistic discipline. Keywords are just one piece of the puzzle. If you want sustainable rankings, you need to think bigger.

When Keywords Ruin Content Quality

I've seen countless landing pages stuffed with keywords. The text looks unnatural, almost robotic. From a pure SEO standpoint, it might give a tiny boost, but it destroys the user experience. Visitors can tell when content is written for bots, not for humans.

At LatSEO, we believe in creating content that serves the reader first. Keywords should fit naturally into the narrative, not the other way around.

Proving SEO Is More Than Keywords

One of the tools I use to demonstrate this is the Glippy GEO extension. It helps me check how a page performs in AI-driven search engines and LLMs (like ChatGPT or Perplexity). In today's world, being found by these systems is just as important as ranking on Google.

When I show clients how GEO optimization can increase their visibility in AI answers, they start to understand that SEO is about being present everywhere, not just in a keyword report.

Technical SEO Beats Keyword Stuffing

Let me give you a concrete example: technical SEO. This is the foundation of everything. Without a clean site structure, Google's crawlbots can't index your pages properly. If the technical base is broken, keywords have little to no effect.

I've seen projects where fixing site architecture, improving page speed, and adding structured data outperformed any keyword tweak. That's why we at LatSEO always start with a technical audit before touching content.

The Right Metric to Show Clients

When a client still believes keywords are the only thing that matters, I show them one simple metric: the average position for the entire website. This gives a bird's-eye view of how the whole machine is working, not just individual parts.

If the average position improves across all pages, it proves that the entire SEO strategy is effective, not just a few keyword-targeted articles.

Conclusion: SEO Is a Symphony, Not a Solo

SEO is like conducting an orchestra. Keywords are the violins, technical SEO is the rhythm section, and content quality is the melody. You need all of them working together to create a masterpiece.
