What is SEO optimisation?

SEO optimisation means putting a website's technical condition, content and external links in order so that Google shows it to people searching for your service. For a local business, it also includes Google Business Profile and visibility on Google Maps.

In practice the work splits into four parts. Technical SEO makes sure Google can read and index the pages. Content answers the questions customers type into the search engine. Links from other websites show Google that your site is trusted. Local SEO helps you appear when a customer searches for a service nearby.

The audit sets the order of work: first I fix the errors that block rankings, and only then work on content and links.

What SEO optimisation includes

SEO optimisation consists of four parts: a technical SEO audit, local SEO (Google Business Profile and Google Maps), content strategy and link building.

Technical SEO audit

Indexing errors, Core Web Vitals, page speed and security. The result is a report with a task list ranked by importance.

How I work on SEO

I use Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, Screaming Frog and Ahrefs. I open Search Console every morning — it shows how impressions and clicks are changing for the whole domain, not just the position of a single keyword.

  1. AuditI crawl the website the way Google sees it and collect errors, rankings and competitor data.
  2. PlanI rank the tasks by importance — from errors that block rankings to smaller improvements.
  3. ImplementationI fix the code, content and platform settings.
  4. ReviewAfter the fixes I check the website again and track rankings and clicks.
