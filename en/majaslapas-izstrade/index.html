Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ About Website Development

How long does it take to develop a website?+

A business card site can be developed in 3–5 business days. A company website takes 2–4 weeks, and an e-commerce store — 4–8 weeks depending on functionality scope and content readiness.

Will the website be SEO optimized?+

Yes, every website we build is developed according to SEO best practices from the ground up — clean code, fast loading (Core Web Vitals), mobile compatibility, optimized URL structure and proper heading hierarchy.

What platforms do you use?+

We work with WordPress, Shopify, and Webflow. We also build fully custom solutions using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The platform is chosen based on your business needs and budget.

Can I edit content myself after development?+

Yes, we provide an easy-to-use CMS admin panel that allows you to add and edit content, images and products without technical knowledge. We also provide training after handover.
