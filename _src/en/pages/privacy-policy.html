Privacy policy and cookies

This page explains what personal data I process when you visit latseo.com or fill in the contact form, and how you can change your cookie choice. Updated: 16 November 2026.

Who is the controller

The data controller is Baltic SEO, SIA (brand LatSEO), reg. no. 40203749304, Kārklu iela 4, Odukalns, Ķekava municipality, LV-2123, Latvia. For any data questions, write to sales@latseo.com.

Contact form and communication

When you fill in the contact form, I receive your name, phone number, email, website address and message. I use this data only to reply to your request and to prepare an offer or a free audit.

  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • How the data is sent: the form first sends the data to a Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) server, which checks it and forwards it without storing it. The data then goes to the automation tool Make.com (EU data centre), which forwards it to my email.
  • Bot protection: the form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which loads only when you start filling in the form. To tell a person from a bot, Cloudflare processes technical data (IP address and browser data). This is a necessary security function (legitimate interest, GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), so it does not depend on cookie consent. Turnstile does not use advertising cookies.
  • Submission limit: to prevent abuse, your IP address is used temporarily, for up to 1 hour, to limit the number of submissions (no more than 5 per hour).
  • Retention: if we do not start working together, I delete the request no later than 12 months after our last contact. If we do, I keep the data for the duration of the contract and as long as required by law (for example, for accounting records).

If you contact me by phone, WhatsApp or email, I use that data for the same purpose and keep it for the same period.

Cookies and analytics

The website works without cookies. Analytics and advertising tools store cookies only if you choose “Accept all” in the cookie notice. If you choose “Necessary only”, none of the tools below store cookies.

I use Google Consent Mode v2. Until you consent, no cookies are stored for Google and only anonymous cookieless signals are sent (for example, that a page was opened). The legal basis for analytics and ad measurement is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).

Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage entry “latseo-consent”). You can change or withdraw it at any time via “Cookie settings” in the page footer.

Analytics data is kept according to the tools' settings: Google Analytics for no longer than 14 months, Microsoft Clarity for no longer than 13 months.

Who else may access the data

  • Google (Google Analytics, Google Ads) and Microsoft (Clarity) — as analytics providers, if you have chosen “Accept all”.
  • Cloudflare — to check, protect from bots and forward contact form data (the data is not stored).
  • Make.com — to forward contact form data.
  • GitHub — website hosting. Like any server, it technically processes your IP address to deliver the page.

Some of these companies may also process data outside the European Economic Area (USA). In that case, the transfer is based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. I do not sell your data or share it for other marketing purposes.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to your data, its correction or deletion, to restrict or object to processing, and to receive your data in a portable format. You can withdraw cookie consent at any time. Send requests to sales@latseo.com; I will reply within one month.

If you believe your data is processed incorrectly, you can lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (www.dvi.gov.lv).
