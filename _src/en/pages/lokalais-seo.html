Local SEO and Google Business Profile

When a customer searches for a service nearby, Google shows a map with three businesses above the regular results. Local SEO in Latvia means setting up your profile, website and reviews so that you appear there.

What I do

What local SEO consists of

To appear at the top of Google Maps, creating a profile is not enough. The profile, website and reviews have to work together.

01

Google Business Profile

The right categories and keywords, a list of services, photos and opening hours.

02

Local keywords

Phrases that people in your city or district search for, for example “zobārsts centrā” (dentist in the centre) or “auto serviss Pārdaugavā” (car service in Pārdaugava).

03

Review system

Automated review requests — email, an SMS reminder or a QR code at your physical location.

04

NAP in directories

The same name, address and phone number (NAP) in all local directories and databases.

05

Local links

Links from regional media, blogs and partners that confirm the business's connection to the region.

06

Localised content

Website pages that match local search intent — the service and the location on one page.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about local SEO

How soon can I expect the first results?

The first changes in Google Maps rankings are usually visible within 2–4 weeks of optimising the profile. The full effect comes within 2–3 months, once the review profile and local authority have stabilised.

Do I need a physical address or office?

Google requires an address to verify a Google Business Profile. Businesses that work at the customer's location can use a service area profile (Service Area Business) with a hidden address. I will help you choose what suits your situation best.

Why do competitors with worse reviews rank higher?

Reviews are only one of the Google Maps ranking factors. Often the deciding factors are profile optimisation, NAP consistency, local links and the website's local content. In the audit I assess all of these factors together.

How do I get more reviews?

I set up automated review requests — an email campaign, SMS reminders or a QR code at your location. I adapt the system to your customer flow.
