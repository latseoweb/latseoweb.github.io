SEO content strategy and SEO copywriting

Design attracts attention, but content decides whether Google shows the page and whether the visitor gets in touch. A content strategy starts with what your customers type into the search engine and ends with a page that answers it.

What a content strategy includes

01

Search intent

I find out what the searcher wants to see — information, a comparison, or a price and a way to sign up — and build the page for that purpose.

02

Keyword research

Using Google Keyword Planner and Search Console data, I find the phrases your customers search for in Latvian and their search volume.

03

Topic clusters

An interlinked page structure: one main page on a topic, with articles leading to it through internal links.

04

E-E-A-T signals

Author information, examples of experience and sources — so that Google and the reader can see who wrote the content and why it can be trusted.

05

Updating existing pages

I find pages that appear in Google but get no clicks and adapt them to the latest search data.

06

Readability and call to action

Headings, lists and short paragraphs that are easy to scan, and a clear next step — a call, a form or a price.

Example

Why impressions without clicks are a content problem

Search Console data for the LatSEO website over 90 days (24 Jul–21 Oct 2026): for the query “seo pakalpojumi” (SEO services) — 1,674 impressions and an average position of 7.7, but 0 clicks. Google was already showing the page on the first page, but the title and description did not persuade people to click.

These are exactly the pages a content audit finds: the ranking is there, but a specific offer, a price or an answer to the question is missing right in the search result.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about SEO content

How is a content strategy different from writing copy?

The strategy determines which pages and topics are needed, which queries each page targets and how the pages link to each other. The copy is the execution of that plan.

Do you always need to write new content?

No. Updating existing pages often has a bigger effect — especially pages that Google already shows but that get no clicks.

Is content strategy part of monthly SEO?

Yes, content optimisation is part of monthly SEO, which costs from €300/month.

How is content quality assessed?

According to Google's helpful content guidelines: whether the content answers the user's question, whether it contains real experience and whether the page is easy to use.
