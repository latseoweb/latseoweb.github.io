How I build links

I follow Google's spam policies. Every link is earned through real partnerships and content.

Topical relevance

A link from a car blog will not help a medical clinic. I select sources based on your niche.

Source quality

I assess a site's authority (Domain Rating) and organic traffic, not just whether a link can be obtained.

Natural anchor text

Too many keywords in links can do harm. I keep a natural balance between the brand name and search phrases.

Content worth referencing

Articles and materials that other websites want to publish or cite — so the link appears in a natural context.

    Disavow. The content is not made for people, and the link is not a real recommendation.

Questions about link building

What is a backlink?

A backlink is a link from another website to your website. Google treats it as a recommendation: the more relevant and trustworthy the source, the more the link is worth.

Can links be bought?

Links bought to influence rankings go against Google's spam policies and can harm a website. I work only with partnerships and content.

How many links do you need?

There is no universal number. It depends on your competitors' link profiles in your niche — I compare them in the audit.
