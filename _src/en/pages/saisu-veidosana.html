Frequently asked questions

Questions about link building

What is a backlink?

A backlink is a link from another website to your website. Google treats it as a recommendation: the more relevant and trustworthy the source, the more the link is worth.

Can links be bought?

Links bought to influence rankings go against Google's spam policies and can harm a website. I work only with partnerships and content.

How many links do you need?

There is no universal number. It depends on your competitors' link profiles in your niche — I compare them in the audit.
