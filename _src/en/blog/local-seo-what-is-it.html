Local SEO means setting up your website and your Google Business Profile so that Google shows you to people searching for your services in a specific city or nearby. Every business needs it if customers come to a physical location or if it travels to customers within a specific region.

I will start without complicated marketing terms. Let's say your car breaks down on the highway near Sigulda, or you want a good coffee in the centre of Riga. Most likely you will take out your phone and type “tow truck in Sigulda” or “good coffee near me” into Google.

In the results, you will most likely pick one of the first options that appear together with a map, reviews and opening hours. That is exactly where local SEO is at work.

What exactly is local SEO?

Local SEO (Search Engine Optimization) means setting up your website and your business's online profile. The goal is for Google to show you to the people who are searching for your services or products in a specific geographic location.

Classic SEO tries to make an article like “how to choose a mattress” visible across the whole country or even the world. Local SEO focuses on making your bed store in Liepāja appear first when someone in Liepāja searches for “buy a mattress”.

Why it matters

  • Searches with local intent. Many people use Google to find real places to go to, or tradespeople to call out to their home. They need a result nearby, not one from across the country.
  • The phrases “near me” and “open now”. Queries like these show that the person wants to solve their need right now and right where they are.
  • Readiness to act. Someone searching for “dentist in the centre” is not just reading about toothache. They are already ready to book an appointment, so a visitor like this is more likely to become a customer.

How does local SEO work?

Google usually knows where the searcher is, for example from the device's location or IP address. For Google to recommend you to local searchers, three things matter.

Google itself explains that local results are determined by three factors: how well the business matches the search, how far it is from the searcher and how well known it is. You cannot change the distance, but you can improve relevance and prominence with the things below.

Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the very foundation. It is a free profile that lets your business appear in Google Maps and at the top of the search results. The block with a map and several businesses above the regular results is called the Local Pack.

Fill in everything in the profile: the exact address, opening hours, phone number, website link and photos. I described it step by step in the article on how to create a Google Business Profile.

Reviews

Google also takes into account what other people say about you. Real, positive reviews on your Google profile are one of the signals Google uses to evaluate a business in local results. Reviews also build trust with the customer who is choosing between several businesses.

Local keywords and website content

Your website's texts should make it clear where you are and what you do. For example: “We offer accounting services in Jelgava.” If the website is also technically in good order, Google understands it more easily — read more about this on the technical SEO page.

Who needs local SEO?

The short answer: any business that customers visit at a physical location, or that travels to customers within a specific region. The most typical examples:

  • Restaurants, cafés and bars. People regularly search for places to eat based on where they are.
  • Medical and beauty services. Dentists, hair salons, massage salons and physiotherapists, which people usually look for near where they live or work.
  • Service providers and tradespeople. Plumbers, electricians, locksmiths and cleaning services. When a problem comes up at home, people look for someone who can come quickly.
  • Physical stores. From flower shops to building supply stores.
  • Lawyers, notaries and real estate agents.

Who it is not the main priority for

If you sell digital products, such as online courses, or ship goods around the world, and you have no store or office where you receive customers, local SEO will not be your priority. Classic or e-commerce SEO will be more useful to you. You can see the available services and prices on the “Services and prices” page.

In summary

Local SEO is not magic, and it takes a bit of work and patience. Still, it is one of the most logical and cheapest ways to attract customers. If your business works in one of the industries above but you are not visible in Google Maps and local searches, those customers go to the competitors who are visible.

Start with the simplest step: create or tidy up your Google Business Profile. It is free and does not take much time.
